## 📌 Executive Summary
This report analyzes Adidas sales performance across time, retailers, regions, and sales methods using an interactive Excel dashboard. The analysis highlights strong year-over-year growth, clear regional leaders, retailer-driven revenue concentration, and a balanced omnichannel sales strategy.

Overall, 2021 significantly outperformed 2020, with noticeable growth momentum after early 2021, indicating post-slowdown recovery and improved demand.

---

## 🎯 Goal of the Project
The goal of this project is to analyze Adidas sales data and transform it into meaningful insights that support business decision-making, performance evaluation, and strategic planning using Excel-based dashboards and visualizations.

---

## 🔄 Procedure / Methodology
The analysis was carried out using a structured, end-to-end approach:

### 1. Data Understanding & Cleaning
Reviewed raw sales data, verified data types, handled formatting issues, and ensured consistency across dates, regions, and product categories.

### 2. Data Transformation
Created calculated fields such as total sales, operating profit, and aggregated metrics required for analysis.

### 3. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
Used pivot tables to explore trends across time, regions, retailers, products, and sales methods.

### 4. Visualization Development
Built line charts, bar charts, maps, and donut charts to visually represent trends, comparisons, and distributions.

### 5. Dashboard Design
Integrated all visuals into a single interactive Excel dashboard with slicers for Year, Product, and Region.

### 6. Insight Generation & Reporting
Interpreted visual outputs to derive business insights and actionable recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Objectives of the Analysis
The primary objectives of this analysis are to:
- Understand overall sales trends over time
- Identify top-performing retailers and regions
- Analyze contribution by sales channel
- Provide actionable insights for business decision-making

---

## 🗂️ Dataset Overview
The dashboard is built using transactional Adidas sales data containing:
- Order Date (2020–2021)
- Product Categories (Men’s & Women’s Apparel and Footwear)
- Regions and States (US)
- Retailers
- Sales Method (In-store, Online, Outlet)
- Total Sales and Operating Profit

Filters are provided for Year, Product, and Region to allow dynamic analysis.

---

## 📊 Visualization-Based Insights

### Sales Trend Over Time (USD)
**Visualization:** Line chart showing monthly sales for 2020 and 2021

**Observations:**
- Sales in 2020 remained relatively flat with minor fluctuations
- A sharp increase is observed at the start of 2021
- Peak sales occur around mid-2021
- A slight dip appears toward late 2021, followed by stabilization

**Insight:**  
The strong upward trend in 2021 suggests recovery-driven demand and improved sales execution compared to 2020.

---

### Retailer Sales & Profit Analysis
**Visualization:** Bar chart (Total Sales) with line overlay (Operating Profit)

**Top Retailers by Sales:**
- West Gear
- Foot Locker
- Sports Direct

**Observations:**
- West Gear generates the highest sales and profit
- Walmart and Amazon contribute lower margins despite high volumes
- Profit does not increase proportionally with sales for all retailers

**Insight:**  
Retailer performance varies significantly. High sales volume does not always translate to high profitability, emphasizing the need for margin-focused partnerships.

---

### Regional Sales Distribution
**Visualization:** US map with state-wise sales intensity

**Observations:**
- West and Southeast regions show strong sales concentration
- States such as California and Texas contribute significantly
- Central regions show moderate performance

**Insight:**  
Sales are geographically concentrated. High-performing regions should be prioritized for inventory expansion and targeted marketing investment.

---

### Sales Method Contribution
**Visualization:** Donut chart showing channel-wise sales split

**Sales Mix:**
- In-store: ~40%
- Outlet: ~33%
- Online: ~27%

**Insight:**  
Sales are well-distributed across channels. While In-store remains dominant, the Online channel shows strong potential for scalable growth.

## 💡 Key Business Insights
- 2021 shows strong recovery and growth compared to 2020
- West Gear and Foot Locker are the most valuable retail partners
- Certain retailers generate high sales but relatively lower profits
- West and Southeast regions drive the majority of revenue
- Balanced omnichannel presence reduces channel dependency risk

---

## 🧠 Business Recommendations
1. Focus on high-margin retailers by strengthening partnerships that deliver both sales and profitability.
2. Invest further in West and Southeast regions while optimizing underperforming regions.
3. Increase digital marketing and e-commerce initiatives to grow the online sales channel.
4. Leverage seasonal sales peaks for inventory planning and promotional campaigns.
